The data owner has authorized research use and public release of the farm
records, including original animal IDs and recorded breeding labels.

The releasing author confirmed on October 7, 2026 that data and derived
research outputs may be released under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International (CC BY 4.0), and the R and Python reproduction code under the
MIT License. See `LICENSE.md`, `LICENSE_DATA.md`, and `LICENSE_CODE.txt`.

Attribute the data to the study and the creators listed in the archival
record. A repository uploader or record creator is not a claim of sole
authorship of the underlying study.

Dependencies and any third-party materials retain their own licenses.

Repository-level scientific attribution is separate from hidden file
properties. Removing workbook authors or computer paths does not remove the
study's provenance, assign ownership to a different person or waive attribution.
